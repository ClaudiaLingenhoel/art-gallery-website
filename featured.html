About the Exhibition

DOT DOT DOT explores repetition, rhythm and visual patterns in contemporary digital and analogue art. The exhibition brings together artists working across photography, illustration, pixel art and mixed media, highlighting how simple elements can create complex visual experiences.

The selected works encourage visitors to slow down and discover unexpected details, connections and structures. From geometric compositions to playful experiments with colour and texture, each artwork offers a different perspective on repetition as a creative process.

Designed as an immersive exhibition, the gallery combines large-scale installations with smaller works that invite close observation. Together they create a dialogue between order and chance, precision and imagination.
